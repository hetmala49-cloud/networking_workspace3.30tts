Difference between SSD and HDD.

|====================================|
| Feature      | SSD       | HDD     |
|====================================|
| Cost         | Expensive | Cheaper |
|------------------------------------|
| Durability   | Higher    | Lower   |
|------------------------------------|
| Boot Time    | Shorter   | Longer  |
|------------------------------------|
| Speed        | Faster    | Slower  |
|------------------------------------|
| Physical Work| No        | Yes     |
|====================================|
