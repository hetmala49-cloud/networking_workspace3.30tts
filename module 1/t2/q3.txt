Explain in your own words the primary functions of the CPU and the chipset on a motherboard, and describe how they work together when you open a music app like Spotify.

CPU (Central Processing Unit)-Calculates data: Processes binary code using its arithmetic logic units.
Executes instructions: Runs the step-by-step logic of your operating system and apps.
Processes instructions: it processes info. and transfers it to other components.

Chipset (Motherboard)-Defines features: Determines how many USB ports, M.2 storage slots, and PCIe lanes your motherboard has.
Manages low-speed data: Handles communication for USB ports, audio chips, and SATA drives.
(it also manages communication between the CPU and hardware comp.)

[ Solid State Drive (SSD) ]->[ Motherboard Chipset ]->[ CPU ]->[GPU]->[Audio Jack ]->[speaker]
