its comp.-

Fans: Its the common used factor to move hot air out and keep the system cool also its mandatory on a basis computer system.

Heatsink: A block of metal made of copper or aluminum is used on CPU or GPU which spread hot air and fans move it out.

Thermal Paste: it is used on cpu or between cpu/gpu and the heat sink which absorbs heat and keeps it cool.

Liquid Cooling Pump & Block: Used in liquid cooling setups instead of a traditional heatsink. The block sits on the CPU to absorb heat, and the pump circulates liquid to carry that heat away.

Radiator: A large metal grid used in liquid cooling. The heated liquid flows through it, and fans blow through the grid to cool the liquid down before it loops back.
